SWPA 2027 Quick Details

Membership & Registration Information

Professional Member

Description

  • Any person identifying with the discipline of psychology is eligible.
  • Professional members receive full association rights and privileges.

Costs

  • $120 — Early Bird (through Jan 25, 2027)
  • $130 — Regular (Jan 26–Mar 15, 2027)
  • $150 — On Site

Professional Member Seeking CE Credits

Description

  • Professional members seeking Continuing Education Credits should register under this category.

Costs

  • $220 — Early Bird (through Jan 25, 2027)
  • $230 — Regular (Jan 26–Mar 15, 2027)
  • $250 — On Site

Student Member

Description

  • Any student identifying with the discipline of psychology is eligible.
  • Student members must document current student status.
  • Student members may not hold offices other than Student Representative.

Costs

  • $70 — Early Bird (through Jan 25, 2027)
  • $80 — Regular (Jan 26–Mar 15, 2027)
  • $100 — On Site

Cancellation Policy

Individuals who pre-register may be eligible for full or partial refunds.

  • Full refund if requested by February 27, 2027.
  • 50% refund if requested between February 28 and March 15, 2027.
  • No refunds after March 15, 2027.

Guest Pass Policy

Types of Guest Passes

  • Weekend Guest Passes
  • Single-Event Guest Passes

Weekend Guest Passes

  • Available online and on-site.
  • Intended for family members supporting conference participants.
  • Can be purchased during registration or added later.
  • Members should provide names of guests.

Single-Event Guest Passes

  • Available on-site only.
  • Grants access for 90 minutes.
  • Cost: $20 per pass.

Questions? Contact SWPA: CONTACT US

Abstract & Programming Submission Information

Posters and Talks

Abstracts must include:

  1. Problem
  2. Method
  3. Results
  4. Conclusions

Abstracts should include citations but not reference lists. No tables or figures.

Abstract limit: 500 words (title and authors excluded).

Maximum title length: 12 words.

Talks should be approximately 15 minutes including Q&A.

Presentation Categories

Empirical Reports

  • Original research
  • Includes data collection, analysis, interpretation
  • Applicable to Talks and Posters

Theoretical Contributions

  • Theoretical analysis
  • No data collection required
  • Not eligible for student awards

Research Proposals

  • Proposed methodology for significant issues
  • Not eligible for student awards
  • Exclusive to Posters

Student Award Submissions

  • Follow existing competition procedures
  • Graduate or undergraduate categories
  • Finalists present talks
  • More info: Student Competitions

Symposia, Workshops & Special Meetings

Symposia

Symposium submissions must include:

  1. Title (12-word max)
  2. 50-word symposium abstract
  3. Summaries of each participant (combined max 500 words)

All participants must be SWPA members.

Workshops & Special Meetings

SWPA provides rooms for workshops and special meetings.

Some workshops may qualify for CE credit.

Contact Program Chair Dr. Tom Faulkenberry HERE

Workshop submissions must include:

  1. Title (12-word max)
  2. Workshop abstract (max 500 words)

Teaching Demonstrations

Follow workshop submission guidelines.
