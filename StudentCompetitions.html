Student Research Competitions at SWPA

Currently enrolled students may submit their original research as entries in the Psi Chi Regional Research Award Competition or the SWPA Student Research Competition. All competition finalists are scheduled for talks in the SWPA program.

These competitions are separate, and authors must indicate which competition they are submitting to.

Psi Chi Regional Research Award Competition

Psi Chi members should submit their abstracts to the Psi Chi competition during the submission process. Psi Chi awards at least twelve $400 Regional Research Awards, the largest monetary award presented at SWPA.

Award recipients may be scheduled for:

  • 15-minute talk
  • Poster presentation
  • Data blitz session

Submissions not selected for Psi Chi awards are automatically forwarded to the SWPA Student Research Competition.

Questions? Contact Dr. Karenna Malavanti: southwesternvp@psichi.org

During submission, Psi Chi members should select the appropriate checkboxes indicating:

  • They wish to submit to the Psi Chi program
  • They are Psi Chi members
  • They wish to enter the Psi Chi Competition

SWPA Student Research Competition

Students who are not Psi Chi members should submit their abstracts to the SWPA Student Research Competition. This competition grants twelve $100 Finalist Awards.

Finalists are scheduled for 20-minute talks judged by SWPA professional members.

One graduate and one undergraduate winner will each receive an additional $100 Best Project Award.

Research Showcase Posters

High-quality submissions not selected as finalists may be chosen for the SWPA Student Research Showcase Poster session.

  • Certificate
  • $25 award
  • Special Psi Chi badge for Psi Chi members

General SWPA Student Posters

Submissions not selected for awards may still be accepted for general poster sessions.

Notice: Students will receive an email indicating their submission category.

Possible Assignment Categories

  1. Finalist
  2. Research Showcase Poster
  3. General Student Poster
  4. Not Accepted

Questions? Contact Dr. Shawn Charlton: scharlton@uca.edu

Guidelines for Submissions

Submission guidelines are identical for both competitions. A faculty sponsor is required but should not be listed as a co-author. The abstract and presentation must be the student’s own work.

At least one author must have paid their student registration fee to submit. All authors must be registered to attend.

Submission Requirements

  1. Submissions must report empirical work.
    • Research proposals are not eligible.
  2. Submissions must include four clearly delineated sections:
    1. Problem: Importance to current literature
    2. Method: Procedure used to collect data
    3. Results: Final or preliminary findings (no tables or figures)
    4. Conclusions: Relevance and implications
  3. Submissions must be 450–500 words.
    • Longer submissions will not be considered.
    • Previously published or presented work is ineligible.

Psi Chi and SWPA submissions are reviewed together using a shared rubric.

Policies for Competitions

  • Competitions may be canceled if insufficient entries are received.
  • Psi Chi submissions not selected for awards automatically enter the SWPA competition.
  • The Awards Committee selects 12 finalists. Non-finalists may be selected for the Undergraduate Research Showcase.

Showcase Criteria

  • Must meet all submission guidelines
  • Must pose a novel question in the Problem section
  • Must describe a suitable research design
  • Must present preliminary data or a clear analysis plan
  • Must link results to future research
  • Must be well-written

Award Levels

Psi Chi Regional Research Awards

  • At least 12 awards
  • $400 each
  • Scheduled for talk, poster, or data blitz

SWPA Student Research Competition Awards

  • 12 finalists
  • $100 each
  • 20-minute judged talk
  • Graduate + Undergraduate Best Project Awards (+$100 each)

Undergraduate Research Showcase Posters

  • $25 award
  • Certificate
  • Poster badge for Psi Chi members

General Student Posters

Accepted submissions not selected for awards.

Principles of Fair Judging

  • At least two judges evaluate all submissions.
  • Judges with conflicts of interest must recuse themselves.
  • Bias concerns should be reported to the Awards Committee Chair.
