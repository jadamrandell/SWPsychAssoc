Mission

As part of an effort to better communicate with our members and provide them with information and resources, SWPA began publishing quarterly e-newsletters in 2008.

These newsletters contain regional research highlights, professional psychology columns, student perspectives, past presidents’ reflections, an events calendar, member news, and more.

All current members receive email links to new issues. Please share the newsletter with colleagues!

Newsletter Content

The newsletter is published three times a year and features:

  • Letter from the President & Officer Updates
  • Featured Articles on Research, Practice, or Teaching
  • Graduate Programs in Focus
  • Columns from State Representatives & Student Representatives

Contact Information

If you have material to submit for an upcoming newsletter or suggestions for research to highlight, please contact the newsletter coordinator, Dr. Jeff Seger.
