SWPA Membership

JOIN SWPA

Beginning in 2016, SWPA began charging one fee for both membership and convention registration. The fee depends on the membership category (Professional or Student) and the payment period (Early-Bird, Regular, or On-Site).

Any person who, by virtue of employment, education, and/or professional interests, identifies with the discipline of psychology is eligible for membership and to attend the conference.

All members receive the SWPA e-newsletter and occasional correspondence from the Executive Committee. Members may also place announcements (non-profit) in the SWPA e-newsletter and on the SWPA website free of charge.

Membership & Registration Categories

Professional Member

Description

  • Any person identifying with the discipline of psychology is eligible.
  • Professional members are entitled to all rights and privileges of the association.

Costs

  • $120 — Early Bird (through Jan 25, 2026)
  • $130 — Regular (Jan 26–Mar 15, 2026)
  • $150 — On Site

Professional Member Seeking CE Credits

Description

  • Any person identifying with the discipline of psychology is eligible.
  • Professional members receive full rights and privileges.
  • This category is for those seeking Continuing Education Credits.

Costs

  • $220 — Early Bird (through Jan 25, 2026)
  • $230 — Regular (Jan 26–Mar 15, 2026)
  • $250 — On Site

Student Member

Description

  • Any student identifying with the discipline of psychology is eligible.
  • Student members must document current student status.
  • Student members may not hold offices other than Student Representative.

Costs

  • $70 — Early Bird (through Jan 25, 2026)
  • $80 — Regular (Jan 26–Mar 15, 2026)
  • $100 — On Site

Cancellation Policy

Individuals who pre-register for the convention may be eligible for full or partial refunds.

  • Full refund if requested by February 27, 2025.
  • 50% refund if requested between February 28 and March 15, 2025.
  • No refunds after March 15, 2025.
